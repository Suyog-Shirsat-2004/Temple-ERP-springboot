Temple History

Trimbakeshwar is one of the most sacred temples in India, dedicated to Lord Shiva, standing as a testament of divine devotion and ancient architectural brilliance.

  • One of the 12 Jyotirlinga temples
  • Located in Nashik, Maharashtra
  • Renowned for its spiritual and cultural heritage

Sacred Deity

The presiding deity is Lord Trimbakeshwar, an avatar of Lord Shiva. The Jyotirlinga holds immense spiritual significance for devotees worldwide.

  • Lord Shiva as the main deity
  • The lingam represents the cosmic pillar (Stambha)
  • A center of faith, rituals, and devotion

Major Festivals

  • Mahashivratri - Celebrated with great devotion
  • Shravan Month - Monsoon celebrations
  • Navratri - Nine divine nights
  • Kartik Poornima - Full moon devotion

Pooja Services

  • Rudrabhishek - Sacred ritual
  • Mahashivratri Pooja - Night vigil
  • Laghu Rudra - Special ceremony
  • Daily Sevas - Regular offerings
Simhastha Kumbh Mela

Nashik–Trimbakeshwar Kumbh Mela 2026–2028

A multi-year sacred gathering at Nashik (Ramkund/Panchvati ghats) and Trimbakeshwar (Kushavarta Kund), spanning October 31, 2026 to July 24, 2028 (about 21 months) with the core ritual cycle in 2026–2027 and major Amrit (Shahi) Snan dates in August–September 2027.

Festival span
31 Oct 2026 – 24 Jul 2028
Peak ritual window
August–September 2027
Key Snan locations
Ramkund, Panchvati, Kushavarta Kund (Trimbakeshwar)
Planning note
Check NTKMA & local Panchang for exact Muhurat timings

Major Amrit (Shahi) Snan & Auspicious Snan Dates — 2027

Peak crowd period: August–September 2027. Dates are for pilgrim planning; confirm final Muhurat/arrangements with authorities.

2 Aug 2027First Shahi (Amrit) Snan
Marks the official start of Kumbh Mela activities. Largest Naga Sadhu procession; massive crowds at Ramkund and Trimbakeshwar.
19 Aug 2027Shravan Purnima Snan
Trimbakeshwar — heavy footfall at Kushavarta Kund.
31 Aug 2027Second Shahi (Amrit) Snan
Shravan Amavasya — major Akhada processions and holy dips.
2 Sep 2027Bhadrapad Amavasya Snan
Highly auspicious; large-scale crowd management.
11 Sep 2027Third Shahi (Amrit) Snan (Ramkund)
Bhadrapada Ekadashi — one of the most important bathing days.
12 Sep 2027Shahi Snan at Trimbakeshwar
Major Shaiva Akhada procession; millions gather at Kushavarta Kund.
17 Sep 2027Bhadrapad Purnima Snan
Full-moon bathing; expanded medical and safety services.

Preparation Timeline (Oct 2026 – Jun 2027)

October 2026 — Construction of Akhada camps and tent cities begins; major infrastructure work (roads, parking zones, lighting, water systems).
November 2026 — Final allocation of Akhada camp zones; cleaning drives at Ramkund, Trimbakeshwar, Panchvati ghats; drainage and sanitation upgrades near Snan areas.
December 2026 — 30–40% of tent city infrastructure completed; temporary electrical line installation begins.
January 2027 — Traffic management plan implemented; CCTV installation and lighting upgrades; final preparation of Akhada Marg and ghat entry routes.
February 2027 — Naga Sadhu diksha preparations begin; Snan schedules allocated to Akhadas; water and mobile toilet facilities become operational.
March 2027 — Pandal, rest house, and medical tent construction begins; yatri camps nearly complete; fire-safety and disaster-management teams deployed.
April 2027 — PM–CM level monitoring and surveillance; dedicated zones for foreign travelers and media; trial runs for major Shahi Snan processions.
May 2027 — Grand entry of Akhadas into the Kumbh area; tight security deployment and crowd-control units activated.
June 2027 — Full-scale religious and administrative activity expansion; significant increase in pilgrim footfall.

Oct–Dec 2027 & Closing Phase (2028)

October 2027 — Auspicious Snan yog at Ramkund and Panchvati; controlled entry/exit routes at ghats.
November 2027 — Maha Yagna and Ratri Diksha ceremonies; cultural programs and devotional performances.
December 2027 — Continued tirthyatras across Nashik and Trimbakeshwar; holy dips and temple darshan remain open.
January 2028 — Makarsankranti dips; additional arrangements.
February 2028 — Expanded crowd; essential services continue.
March 2028 — Purnima Snan; Hanuman Jayanti fairs at Ramkund.
April 2028 — Chaitra Purnima — Antim Pramukh Snan (final major bath).
May–June 2028 — Akhadas depart; administrative dismantling and restoration.
24 July 2028 — Closure — Guru exits Simha Rashi; Kumbh officially concludes.
Key takeaways for pilgrims: Major Snan dates (2, 19, 31 Aug; 2, 11, 12, 17 Sep 2027). Best quieter window: late 2026–early 2027 before peak footfall. Always verify exact Muhurat/entry rules with NTKMA and local authorities.
